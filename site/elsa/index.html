Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

Does Elsa tell people she's an AI?

Yes. Before you tap to talk, the screen says Elsa is an AI receptionist.

What if Elsa can't answer a question?

She won't guess or make things up. She takes the caller's name, contact details and the best time to call, then passes it to you so you can get back to them.

Is the chat recorded?

Elsa doesn't record audio. After every chat, you get an email with a summary, what the caller needs, their contact details, and the time and length of the call. If you'd like a written transcript too, just ask.

Can Elsa answer questions about my hours and service area?

Yes. When we set her up, we take the details from your website and anything else you want her to know. She sticks to what you've given her. She's on 24/7, so callers get answers after hours too. If she doesn't know, she takes their details for you.

Can Elsa flag urgent enquiries?

Yes. If a caller says it's urgent, your email is marked URGENT so it stands out. Elsa can also give them your phone number, if you'd like her to.
